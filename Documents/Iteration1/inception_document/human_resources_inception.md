# Inception: Human Resources

Human Resources supplies the people who keep the airline and airport running: no flight can depart without pilots and flight attendants, no aircraft can return to service without maintenance technicians, and no passenger can board without gate agents and baggage handlers. The Human Resources department hires employees into these roles, keeps a record of each employee's role, hire date, salary and employment status, assigns crew to flights, and pays employees. It supplies crew to Flight Management for each scheduled flight, maintenance technicians to Aircraft Management, and gate agents and baggage handlers to Airport Operations. Its salary records feed Finance through payroll.

The system is a terminal-based ERP with file-based storage. Payroll calculates tax withholding and benefit deductions; filing with government agencies, benefits enrollment and actual bank transfers are out of scope, as are login, authentication and a GUI. 

**Iteration 1 use case:** Hire Employee

## Brainstormed Human Resources use cases

1. Hire Employee (Iteration 1)
2. Assign Employee to Flight (Iteration 2, subject to change)
3. Process Payroll (Iteration 3, subject to change)
4. Terminate Employee
5. Record Leave of Absence
6. Update Employee Salary
