# Inception: Ticket Sales

Ticket Sales is the airline's core revenue-generating loop: every other department (Flight Management, Aircraft Management, Human Resources, Airport Operations) exists so seats can be sold and flown. The Ticket Sales department sells seats on scheduled flights to passengers, collects payment, issues tickets, and handles changes, cancellations and refunds after the sale. It depends on Flight Management for the schedule of flights and on Aircraft Management for the aircraft flying each flight. Its sales records feed Airport Operations (check-in and boarding) and Finance.

The system is a terminal-based ERP with file-based storage. Login, authentication and a GUI are out of scope.

**Iteration 1 use case:** Purchase Ticket

## Brainstormed Ticket Sales use cases

3. Process Refund (Iteration 3)
